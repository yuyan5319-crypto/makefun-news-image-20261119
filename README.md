# The World Today — Thursday 19 November 2026 in one image

A make.fun entry for **news-image-20261119** ("Sum up 19 November's world
news in one image", $250).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) draws a newspaper
front page of the day's world news pixel by pixel — its own PNG encoder,
rasteriser and 5x7 bitmap typeface — and writes it to `/output/entry.png`
(locally: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`). The drawing is fully
deterministic: two runs write byte-identical files.

## The day's front page

- **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — the first ever three-way
  meeting of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders opens at the APEC summit;
  nuclear arms, Iran and Ukraine are on the table; officials say no deal
  is expected.
- **World Court rules today** — the ICJ in The Hague delivers its verdict
  on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Gambia
  brought the case in 2019.
- **Tankers hit in Hormuz** — Iran says three tankers caught fire after
  hitting mines east of the strait; a UK body reports another tanker hit
  by a projectile.
- **Jeffries nominated** — House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries
  251-11; he is set to become the first Black Speaker of the US House.
- **GTA VI launches worldwide** — Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI goes on
  sale around the world to near-perfect reviews, returning players to
  Vice City.
- **Kashmir commander killed** — Indian police kill Lashkar commander
  Lateef Bhat in an encounter in Jammu and Kashmir.
- **Voyager 1 passes a milestone** — NASA's Voyager 1, launched in 1977,
  reaches one light-day from Earth, the farthest any human object has
  flown.

In brief: the EU proposes a 45bn euro loan for Ukraine; Cyclone Akvan
nears Andhra Pradesh; the US defence chief visits Colombia after a
Venezuela visit; a Pakistani is jailed 27 years over a Trump murder plot.
